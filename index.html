Clip Studio Clock

A desk display for the Clip Studio Gaming YouTube channel.

What it does

Clip Studio Clock is a small private program that runs on the channel owner's own computer. Every 10 minutes it reads the channel's statistics from YouTube and shows them on a pixel clock on the owner's desk:

  • views gained today and total views, and
  • the share of recent views that come from the United States and Canada.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, it uses two read-only Google permissions: viewing the owner's YouTube account (for view counts) and viewing YouTube Analytics reports (for views by country). It cannot upload, edit, delete or post anything, and it is not offered to anyone else.
